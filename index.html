FAQ

Questions, answered

When will Pulse One ship?

Pre-orders ship in the order they were placed, starting in early spring. We'll email you a tracking link the day yours leaves the warehouse.

Do they work with Android and iPhone?

Yes. Pulse One uses standard Bluetooth and works with any phone, tablet or laptop. The Pulse app is available for both iOS and Android.

Can I use just one earbud?

Absolutely. Either bud works on its own for music and calls, and switches to mono automatically.

What if I lose an earbud?

Find My Pulse in the app shows the last known location of each bud. Pro + Care customers also get a free replacement bud.

What's your return policy?

If you're not in love with Pulse One, return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Return shipping is on us.
